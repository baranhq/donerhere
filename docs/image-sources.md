## Notes

- The owner reports using AI to improve some product photos, but is unsure which files; the AI service is also unknown. Identify those assets and check the applicable image and AI-service terms before commercial publication. Canva's agreement includes an AI/ML restriction for Canva Content, so confirm whether it applies to any Canva item used as AI input.
- The Pexels and Aflo items were shown as free in the Canva source screenshots. Standard [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license) and [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license) images allow commercial use without required attribution. The exact Canva/Aflo license record for the fries image was not captured.
- Each image remains under its source terms; this list records sources and does not claim ownership of third-party photos.
